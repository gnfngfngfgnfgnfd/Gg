# Scrapped Mobs Addon for Minecraft Bedrock Edition

This addon gives **15 scrapped and concept Minecraft mobs** a second chance!

## Mob List

| # | Mob | Type | Description |
|---|-----|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Alpaca** | Passive Animal | Gentle camelid from savanna biomes. Breeds with wheat/hay. Drops wool. |
| 2 | **Red Dragon** | Boss-tier Hostile | The legendary scrapped flying boss. Shoots fireballs, found in mountains. 200 HP. |
| 3 | **The Great Hunger** | Hostile | Ancient subterranean predator. Fast, strong, lurks in caves. |
| 4 | **Barnacle** | Hostile (Aquatic) | Stationary ocean predator with short-range bite. Clings to underwater floors. |
| 5 | **Wildfire** | Hostile (Nether) | Flying Nether enemy surrounded by spinning fire shields. Shoots small fireballs. |
| 6 | **Beaver** | Passive Animal | Semi-aquatic animal found near rivers and forests. Drops logs and leather. |
| 7 | **Glow Worm** | Passive Animal | Tiny bioluminescent cave critter. Drops glow ink sacs. |
| 8 | **Goblin** | Hostile | Small, cunning cave-dwelling mob that attacks players for loot. |
| 9 | **Lava Zombie** | Hostile (Nether) | Fire-immune zombie variant that walks in lava. Weak to water. |
| 10 | **Musician** | Passive NPC | Roaming villager-type mob. Drops music discs and note blocks. |
| 11 | **Shark** | Hostile (Aquatic) | Apex ocean predator. Fast swimmer, 10 attack damage. |
| 12 | **Sloth** | Passive Animal | Extremely slow jungle/forest animal. Breeds with apples. |
| 13 | **Snail** | Passive Animal | Tiny slow creature in forests and plains. Drops slimeballs. |
| 14 | **Troll** | Hostile | Large cave/mountain monster. Tanky, hits hard. |
| 15 | **HeroBrian** (Boss) | BOSS | The ultimate boss. 300 HP, teleports, phases, darkens the sky. Drops nether star + diamonds. Summon only. |

## Spawn Eggs

All mobs (except HeroBrian) have spawn eggs. HeroBrian must be summoned:
```
/summon scrapped:herobrian
```

## Installation

1. Open the `.mcaddon` file with Minecraft
2. Apply both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack to your world
3. Enable **Experimental Gameplay** if prompted

## Notes

- Textures use placeholder colors — add custom textures in `resource_pack/textures/entity/`
- HeroBrian has a boss health bar and darkens the sky on spawn
- The Great Hunger and Wildfire are Nether-tier threats
